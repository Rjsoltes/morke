Mørke

Privacy policy

The short version

Mørke doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings (scores, the run in progress, trophies and preferences) are saved on your iPhone so you can continue where you left off.

Apple services you can choose to use

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, your progress is also kept in your own iCloud account so it can follow you to your other devices. It's stored by Apple under your Apple Account, and the developer can't see it.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center, and your Game Center nickname appears on the leaderboards. Apple's privacy policy covers this: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

Mørke doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
