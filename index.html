Support

Found a bug, or have a question or idea? Open an issue on GitHub and I'll get back to you. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

How to play

  • Slide anywhere to turn the ballista, then tap to fire. Bank shots off the walls.
  • Touch three or more of the same creature to pop them. Anything left hanging drops for big points.
  • Every shot uses a bolt. When the rack runs out, the shadow pushes the board down a row.
  • If the board reaches your line, a phial of starlight goes dark. Lose all three and the run is over.
  • Merlin matches any creature. The Shadow Wraith can't be popped, only dropped.

Questions

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your iPhone as you play, and Continue picks up exactly where you left off. If you're signed in to iCloud, it also follows you to your other devices.

Why don't I see leaderboards?

Leaderboards and achievements use Game Center. Turn it on in Settings › Game Center, then reopen Mørke.

How do I start over?

Open More › Settings and choose Reset all progress. Scores already posted to Game Center stay there.

Does it need the internet?

No. Mørke plays fully offline. Game Center and iCloud catch up the next time you're online.
